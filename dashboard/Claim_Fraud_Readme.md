I created an fraud dashboard summarizing fraud exposure and profitability. The dashboard showed a fraud rate of 10.52%, fraud loss of ₹1.61 crore, and leakage rate of 46.64%, indicating many suspicious claims were still approved. Cancer claims drove the highest fraud loss, and Bengaluru had the highest city-level fraud rate. Claim and loss ratios above 100% highlighted significant underwriting pressure.
